PUBLISH YOUR WEBSITE WITH CLOUDFLARE
No Git or GitHub needed

You can publish your website by uploading a folder to Cloudflare.

1. ASK YOUR CODING AI TO PREPARE THE WEBSITE

Copy this message into the AI you're building with:

"I want to publish my website using Cloudflare Pages' drag-and-drop upload.
Check whether my site supports this. If it does, build the finished website
and show me exactly which folder to upload. Make sure index.html is directly
inside that folder. If it needs a separate server, explain what else is needed."

The finished folder is often called dist, build, or out. Your AI should tell
you exactly which one to use.

2. OPEN CLOUDFLARE

Visit https://dash.cloudflare.com/

Sign in or create an account.

3. CREATE YOUR WEBSITE

Open Workers & Pages, then click Create application.

Find the Pages option, then choose Get started and Drag and drop your files.

Enter a name for your website, such as my-awesome-game.

4. UPLOAD AND PUBLISH

Drag the folder your AI prepared into the upload area.

Upload the finished website folder, not your entire coding project.

Click Deploy site or Save and Deploy.

Cloudflare will give you a public address like:
https://my-awesome-game.pages.dev

Open it, check that your website works, and share the link!

UPDATING YOUR WEBSITE LATER

After making changes, ask your AI:

"Build the latest version of my website for Cloudflare Pages and show me
the folder to upload."

Then:

1. Open your existing website in the Cloudflare dashboard.
2. Click Create a new deployment.
3. Choose Production. This means updating the website everyone visits.
4. Upload the freshly built folder and deploy it.

Your public website address stays the same. You do not need to create a
new website each time you update it.

You can close your computer afterward. Cloudflare keeps the website online.
